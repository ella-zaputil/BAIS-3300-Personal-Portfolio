Recent Work

Civco Chargeback Validation System

I worked with CIVCO to analyze chargeback data using Excel and Power BI, focusing on identifying discrepancies between distributor submissions and internal records. By organizing and visualizing historical data, I highlighted patterns in inconsistencies and areas where the reconciliation process could be improved. The project resulted in clearer reporting, better data accuracy, and more efficient workflows for future chargeback management.

Digital Product Management Professional Portfolio

This digital product management project highlights my ability to design, prototype, and evaluate a user-centered digital experience. Using HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript, I created an interactive product prototype that demonstrates clear navigation, responsive layout, and thoughtful accessibility decisions. Throughout the project, I applied key product management skills such as user research, wireframing, feature prioritization, and iteration based on feedback. I also gained hands-on experience translating product requirements into functional design elements, ensuring the final prototype aligned with user needs and business goals. This project strengthened my understanding of how technical development and strategic product thinking work together to bring a digital product to life.
