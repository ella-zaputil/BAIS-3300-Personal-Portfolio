Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 8, 2025

This privacy policy explains how Ella Zaputil ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit this personal portfolio website (ellazaputil.com).

This policy applies only to data collected through this Site. It does not apply to other websites, services, or platforms that may be linked from this Site.

I aim to keep the Site simple and collect minimal data. The types of information collected are:

Google may process IP addresses and other technical identifiers as part of providing analytics services.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Small text files (cookies) or local storage may be used to support Google Analytics and to improve the Site experience. No other third-party trackers are intentionally used.

No sign-up, contact form, comments, or other interactive features are present on the Site that collect personal contact information unless explicitly provided by you through a direct message or email outside the Site (in which case the data you send will be used only to respond to your message).

I do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

I retain analytics and usage data for as long as necessary to support the purposes described above, subject to any retention settings configured in Google Analytics and applicable legal requirements. Aggregated or anonymized analytics data may be kept for longer-term analysis.

The Site is not intended for children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe I have collected personal information of a child under 13, please contact me so I can delete it.
